How it works

From district data to a storage plan

Start from the district record

Area, production and yield for every season from 2018-19 to 2023-24.

Project the seasons after the record

A linear trend gives the expected {{ forecast_seasons|join(' and ') }} crop, with an honest uncertainty band.

Match demand to capacity

A month-by-month model loads the crop into cold storage and releases it through the year.

Act on the gaps

Deficit months and strained blocks are flagged, so you can plan space, transport or sales early.
